Quick guide
  • Type a task and press Enter. Shortcuts in the text: fri, tomorrow, 3pm, ~45m, @project, #tag, !!.
  • Tap a task to open it: date and time, status (including Waiting), project, estimate, photo, notes, subtasks.
  • Drag the dots on the left of a row to reorder (sort set to Manual).
  • Pin up to 3 tasks to Focus on the Today tab; start a timer from any task.
  • Review tab: your week at a glance.
  • Keys: N new task, / search, 1–6 tabs, Ctrl/Cmd+Enter add, Esc close.
